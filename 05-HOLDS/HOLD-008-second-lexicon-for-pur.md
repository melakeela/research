# HOLD-008 — a lexicon of Vedic or Sanskrit independent of Grassmann, on `púr-`

**Raised:** 2026-09-08, by `06-BRIEFS/childrens-pilot/`, on the fourth
adversarial review pass.
**Blocks:** screen S-02 of the children's investigation at full strength; gate
**G-13**. Constitution §7's *semantic range* requirement for `púr-`.
**Does not block:** the rest of the investigation. Every count survives, because
the counts are measurements over a fixed membership; what does not survive
unqualified is the claim that the membership and the meaning are two things.
**Ledger:** `SRC-089` (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`, 2026-09-08), re-probing `SRC-041`
(`EGRESS_BLOCKED`, 2026-09-07). Both hostnames, both probes, exit 56, 403.

## What is needed

Monier-Williams, Böhtlingk-Roth (the *Petersburger Wörterbuch*), Apte, or
Macdonell's *Vedic Reader* glossary — any lexicon on `púr-` whose authority is
not Grassmann's. One would do.

## Why it is needed, and why it is larger than one screen

`DEP-005`: *"The Zurich lemmatisation and the Grassmann gloss travel together in
the same annotation layer. A claim that cites the lemma and a claim that cites
the meaning are one source, not two."*

`DEP-021` and `PUR-006` add the other half: **the family boundary was fixed by
Grassmann's glosses.** So in this corpus Grassmann supplies (a) which tokens are
`púr-` at all, (b) what `púr-` means, and (c) through `SRC-074`, one of the
translations used to check the other two.

Everything the children's investigation counts — 106 tokens, 103 passages, 83
simplex, 21 counting passages, 10 materials, 93 silences — is a measurement over
a membership Grassmann defined, presented beside a meaning Grassmann wrote.
**That is not a reason to doubt the counts.** It is a reason the screen may not
say *"**the** dictionary says it is a wall of stones and clay"*, which is what
S-02 said for four review passes.

Constitution §7 also asks for a **semantic range**, and one lexicographer's gloss
is not a range. `PUR4J-021` is `VERIFIED` as a statement of what Grassmann
prints, and that is all it is.

## Why this is a HOLD and `HOLD-007` is not

`HOLD-007` records a retrieval **nobody has attempted**. This one records a
retrieval **attempted twice and refused at the proxy** — `SRC-041` on
2026-09-07, `SRC-089` on 2026-09-08, both hostnames, exit 56, CONNECT tunnel
403. `CLAUDE.md` defines `HOLD` as "Blocked on source access", and that is
exactly and only what this is.

**A note for the next reviewer, because a reviewer got this wrong.** `SRC-041`'s
`access_status` reads `VERIFIED`, which means the ledger row is verified — the
probe really happened and its result is recorded. It does **not** mean the source
is reachable. The reachability field is `retrieval_capable`, and on `SRC-041` it
reads **NO**. The fourth adversarial pass read `VERIFIED` as availability and
concluded that a second lexicon was one cheap retrieval away, which would have
made this an unrun retrieval rather than a hold. It was re-probed rather than
taken on trust, and it is blocked.
