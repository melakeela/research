# HOLD-001 — the etymology of púraṃdhi-

**Raised by:** the púr- family search, 2026-09-07
**Blocks:** the family boundary in `03-REGISTERS/rigveda-pur-family.csv`
(claim `PUR-023`), and therefore the token totals in `PUR-005`,
`PUR-014` and `PUR-018`.

## The question

Is `púraṃdhi-` (48 tokens, plus `púraṃdhivant-` 1 and
`smátpuraṃdhi-` 1) a compound of `púr-` "stronghold"?

Grassmann's gloss, as carried in VedaWeb's `matched_lemmata.json`
(SRC-022), is *"etwa: 'Segensfülle, Reichtum'; auch Personifikation
der Segensfülle, Name einer Göttin"* — abundance, not fortification.
The register follows that gloss and excludes the group.

The exclusion is a judgement. An older analysis reads the word as
`púr-` + `√dhā`, "fort-holding", and the surface form supports it as
readily as it supports the abundance reading. The competing modern
derivation is from `*pr̥H-` "to fill" via a `-dhi-` stem, which is
what gives "abundance".

## Why it is on hold rather than decided

Deciding it needs at least one of:

- Mayrhofer, *Etymologisches Wörterbuch des Altindoarischen*, s.v.
  `púraṃdhi-`. Not retrievable in this session.
- Jamison & Brereton 2014, or Geldner's notes on the Puraṃdhi
  passages. Not retrievable in this session.
- Lubotsky's *Rgvedic Word Concordance* headword treatment. The
  VedaWeb clone carries Lubotsky's text but not his concordance
  apparatus.

`archive.org` is reachable as of 2026-09-07 (SRC-025), so a
pre-1930 treatment may be obtainable; Mayrhofer and Jamison &
Brereton are in copyright and are not.

## What the answer would change

Nothing in the direction of the result, and it is the conservative
choice as it stands. Including the group raises the family from 106
to 156 tokens and **strengthens** the Popular-stratum deficit:
χ² rises from 19.98 to 24.29 on 4 df (p = 7.0 × 10⁻⁵), with 5
Popular tokens observed against 21.7 expected.

So the hold does not block `PUR-026`. It is recorded because the
family boundary is a stated methodological choice and must be
auditable, not because the answer is load-bearing.
