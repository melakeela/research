# HOLD-003 — the Para-Munda proposal cannot be quoted from its own statement

**Raised by:** domain E source probe, 2026-09-07
**Blocks:** distinctions 6–9 of constitution §4.E — reconstructed
Proto-Munda, deeper Austroasiatic hypotheses, Witzel's historical
"Para-Munda" proposal, and the weaker Kubhā-Vipāś / unknown-prefixing-
language fallback.
**Ledger:** `SRC-043`, `SRC-045`

## What is needed

The papers in which the proposal is actually made, so that its claims can be
stated in its author's words with a locator, rather than characterised from
memory. Primarily the Electronic Journal of Vedic Studies, the open-access
venue for this material, at `ejvs.laurasianacademy.com`.

Also wanted, and in copyright: Kuiper's *Aryans in the Rigveda* (1991) with
its list of Rigvedic words held to be foreign, and Pinnow's and later
comparative Munda reconstructions.

## Why this is a hold and not a summary

The inheritance rule in `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that a model's recollection
of a prior conversation is not a retrieval, and the same reasoning applies a
fortiori to a model's recollection of a paper. Writing out what Para-Munda
"says" from memory would manufacture exactly the evidentiary standing the
rule exists to withhold, and it would do it for the one hypothesis whose
mishandling the constitution names in §1 as the failure this whole
methodology corrects.

So the Para-Munda proposal is carried in this unit as a **named position
whose content is not yet retrieved**. It is gated at step 7 on that basis:
it is not excluded for being wrong, it is not admitted for being famous, and
it receives no analytical space until its own statement is in hand.

This is a different thing from the Kubhā-Vipāś fallback, which is a
*weaker* claim (an unidentified prefixing language in a named region) and is
gated separately. Collapsing the two is one of the failure modes §4.E
forbids.

## What the answer would change

It would move distinctions 8 and 9 from `HOLD` to a testable state, and it
would supply the word-lists on which any prefixing-language claim rests.
Until then no count in this unit is a count *of* Para-Munda material, and
none is offered as one.
