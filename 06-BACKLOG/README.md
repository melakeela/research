& Institutional Backlog".

## Row set

95 rows: items 1–89 as `BL-001`–`BL-089`, and the six programmes as
`PROG-01`–`PROG-06`.

The six programmes are **unnumbered in the original and placed between items
65 and 66**. The CSV keeps them in that position — `BL-065`, then `PROG-01` to
`PROG-06`, then `BL-066` — and each programme row says so in `reason`. They
are not items 90–95 and do not extend the 1–89 numbering.

### `prior_research_available`

Computed by the builder from `03-REGISTERS/` under two rules, both declared in
the script:

1. **Named registers.** Where a register's own subject matter matches an
   item's title, the register is cited with a one-line statement of what it
   holds — items 2, 9, 10, 11, 13 and 25.
2. **Keyword match over `inherited-claims.csv`.** Row counts and first/last
   claim ids for the rows whose subject matches the item. The patterns are in
   the script and the count is reproducible by re-running it.

Two properties of this column matter:

- It says **where** material bearing on an item sits. It says nothing about
  what that material shows. Every `inherited-claims.csv` row counted is
  `INHERITED-UNVERIFIED`; a count of 21 rows is 21 untested claims, not
  evidence of coverage.
- The mapping is driven by item **titles** and register **contents** only. It
  is deliberately not driven by `EXPANSION-PROMPT-2026-09-07.md`, whose
  per-item prose would otherwise decide these cells by unverified assertion.

Item 1 is the one special case: every one of the 369 `inherited-claims.csv`
rows is `INHERITED-UNVERIFIED` and therefore within the scope of "audit every
load-bearing factual claim", so the row cites the register whole.

`NONE IN 03-REGISTERS/` in 76 rows is a finding, not an omission. Research in
this repository so far is concentrated in Rigvedic and Dravidian-substrate
work; most of the product, learning, visual and institutional items have no
prior research here at all.

### The seven columns left empty

`research_required`, `product_spec_required`, `technical_dependency`,
`institutional_dependency`, `proposed_destination`, `release` and
`final_zip_location` all describe what an item *asks for*. Only its title was
recovered. A title is not a specification: "WATER", "FIELD", "OBJECT",
"PROVE IT" and "Revision history" do not state their own scope, and writing a
plausible scope from a one-line title is invention.

They are left empty and will be filled when the full original text of the 89
items arrives, or item by item as the owner specifies scope.
