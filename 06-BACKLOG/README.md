& Institutional Backlog".

Re-run it from the repository root; do not hand-edit the CSV, or the next run

## Row set

95 rows: items 1–89 as `BL-001`–`BL-089`, and the six programmes as
`PROG-01`–`PROG-06`.

The six programmes are **unnumbered in the original and placed between items
65 and 66**. The CSV keeps them in that position — `BL-065`, then `PROG-01` to
`PROG-06`, then `BL-066` — and each programme row says so in `reason`. They
are not items 90–95 and do not extend the 1–89 numbering.

### `current_site_coverage` and `existing_route` are still not established — but not for the reason first recorded

**Corrected 2026-09-08.** The original wording said the route inventory did
not exist. That was true of the path it was told to look at and false of the
artefact. `06-BRIEFS/SITE-INVENTORY.md` does not exist in *this* repository,
on this branch or any other. `SITE-INVENTORY.md` **does** exist, in
`melakeela/site`: read on 2026-09-08 at commit `e6b6b67`, logged as `SRC-089`,
listing 133 root HTML files, with a published evidence export covering 68 of
them.

What is still not established is the **mapping**: which of those 133 routes
cover which of these 89 titles. Nothing in either repository states it, and
assigning it now would be a new editorial taxonomy rather than an audit
finding — the 2026-09-07 cross-repository audit says so in as many words. So
both columns keep an explicit `NOT ESTABLISHED` marker, which now names where
the inventory is and which decision the mapping waits on (`D-052`), rather
than a blank, so that "we did not establish this" still cannot be misread as
"no page exists". The hold is `MH-005`.

Three artefacts were considered as substitutes and all three were rejected:

- `01-INHERITED/curatorial-audit-v1.1/page-audit.csv` names 96 pages, but it
  is a frozen 2026-09-01 baseline and `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md` **D-033** records
  an open question about whether it describes the live build at all. Using it
  would assert current coverage from a snapshot of unknown currency.
- `03-REGISTERS/` `supports_page` values are mostly marked `(proposed)`. The
  one live page named anywhere in the registers is **`the-killed.html`**
  (`03-REGISTERS/rigveda-pur-family.csv`, `rigveda-pur-family-occurrences.csv`).
  It is recorded here rather than in the CSV, because nothing in the
  repository maps it to a backlog item and choosing one would be a guess.
- `01-INHERITED/claude-project-handoff.md` counts are `INHERITED-UNVERIFIED`
  and mutually inconsistent — `inherited-claims.csv` `IH-250`, `IH-251` and
  `IH-104` are rows recording exactly that disagreement.

Filling 95 rows from any of these would produce the complete-looking,
unsourced CSV that `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md` D-014 and constitution §1 exist to
prevent. `METHODOLOGY-CONSTITUTION.md` §10 is explicit: *"Do not call an item
covered merely because a related phrase appears in another packet."*

**To finish these two columns:** answer `D-052` — who owns the route-to-item
mapping and whether ambiguous rows go to owner review rather than a forced
primary category — and the CSV is regenerated against `SRC-089`, not
rewritten. The route list is no longer the missing input; the mapping is.

### `prior_research_available`

Computed by the builder from `03-REGISTERS/` under two rules, both declared in
the script:

1. **Named registers.** Where a register's own subject matter matches an
   item's title, the register is cited with a one-line statement of what it
   holds — items 2, 9, 10, 11, 13 and 25.
2. **Keyword match over `inherited-claims.csv`.** Row counts and first/last
   claim ids for the rows whose subject matches the item. The patterns are in
   the script and the count is reproducible by re-running it.

Two properties of this column matter:

- It says **where** material bearing on an item sits. It says nothing about
  what that material shows. Every `inherited-claims.csv` row counted is
  `INHERITED-UNVERIFIED`; a count of 21 rows is 21 untested claims, not
  evidence of coverage.
- The mapping is driven by item **titles** and register **contents** only. It
  is deliberately not driven by `EXPANSION-PROMPT-2026-09-07.md`, whose
  per-item prose would otherwise decide these cells by unverified assertion.

Item 1 is the one special case: every one of the 369 `inherited-claims.csv`
rows is `INHERITED-UNVERIFIED` and therefore within the scope of "audit every
load-bearing factual claim", so the row cites the register whole.

`NONE IN 03-REGISTERS/` in 76 rows is a finding, not an omission. Research in
this repository so far is concentrated in Rigvedic and Dravidian-substrate
work; most of the product, learning, visual and institutional items have no
prior research here at all.

### The seven columns left empty

`research_required`, `product_spec_required`, `technical_dependency`,
`institutional_dependency`, `proposed_destination`, `release` and
`final_zip_location` all describe what an item *asks for*. Only its title was
recovered. A title is not a specification: "WATER", "FIELD", "OBJECT",
"PROVE IT" and "Revision history" do not state their own scope, and writing a
plausible scope from a one-line title is invention.

They are left empty and will be filled when the full original text of the 89
items arrives, or item by item as the owner specifies scope.
