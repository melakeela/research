# Release portfolio — corrected v1.1

## Release 1

### Three flagship public builds

1. **Artifact Atlas v2** — typed evidence/movement layers, derived counts, stable records, time/space uncertainty and source drawer.
2. **Keezhadi PROVE IT** — one bounded adult/child investigation asking what a mark can prove.
3. **WATER** — one Living World linking dated environment, settlement, material life, text and memory without continuity inflation.

### Shared enablers

- **Homepage/Veḷi threshold:** one real record opens a question and passes into inspectable evidence.
- **Evidence/source viewer:** locator, role, source ancestry, counter-evidence, uncertainty, revision and what would change the claim.

### Audience proof

- **Child/Field Bag slice:** the same Keezhadi evidence through different actions; local-only storage and two child tests.
- **Minimal classroom packet:** one account-free 45–60 minute inquiry with group roles and accessible export.

### Safety work

- Correct the ten ranked claim clusters across page, metadata, search, Atlas and related links.
- Complete rights, consent, accessibility, privacy and security review.

### Parallel research—not a launch gate

- Brahui/Kurukh/Malto and broader North Dravidian work continues against Domain M blockers, digitisation lineage, corpus parity and community/audio requirements. It becomes a Release 1 dependency only if the owner explicitly chooses that trade-off.

## Acceptance tests

| Role | Acceptance test |
|---|---|
| Atlas flagship | One approved derived count; every map mark has record/type/date/uncertainty/source; eight layers are separable; no object implies language; unknown zones render. |
| Keezhadi flagship | Adult and child can state observation, supported claim, complication, uncertainty and what would change it; no literacy/language overclaim. |
| WATER flagship | Every link in the chain is dated/sourced or shown as unknown; periods do not merge. |
| Threshold enabler | A first-time visitor can explain MelaKeela versus Veḷi and reach an investigation from one object/question. |
| Viewer enabler | Ten audited fixtures render locator, role, ancestry, counter, uncertainty and revision losslessly. |
| Audience validation | Two child tests and one educator pilot pass without accounts, diluted evidence or behavioural profiling. |
| Safety | No superseded statement remains in HTML, metadata, search index or related surfaces; all assets/data flows have rights/accessibility status. |

Release 1 is not a 133-page redesign and not 47 equal builds. Retained routes remain contextual and are curated behind the proof experiences.
